Visit us · Al Khawaneej, Dubai

Come in, stay a while.

A flower shop and café in Al Khawaneej, where stems are chosen with intention and coffee is served in a warm, charming atmosphere.

The café

A room made for lingering.

Two childhood friends imagined a flower shop that feels private and welcomes its neighbours. Today you can browse fresh-cut stems, sip coffee, and sit in a warm space with a view of the arrangements being made.
