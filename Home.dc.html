02 — The story

It began with two friends. And a love of flowers.

Over a decade ago, two childhood friends shared a dream.

Inspired by their love of freshly cut flowers and the emotions they evoke, they imagined a different kind of flower shop.

One that felt personal. One that welcomed its neighbours. One where every arrangement carried a little more meaning.

That dream became Côté Fleurs.

Today, you can enjoy the beauty of the flowers, taste delicious drinks and settle into a warm atmosphere.

03 — The art of flowers

A little wild. Entirely beautiful.

Our arrangements embrace the natural romance of flowers. Wild in spirit, thoughtful in composition and carefully finished by hand.

04 — From farm to flower

Beautiful things begin at the source.

From Ecuador and Kenya to the Netherlands, we source exceptional flowers directly from carefully selected growers around the world.

Our relationships with these farms allow us to offer distinctive varieties, remarkable freshness and an evolving selection of seasonal blooms.

Every stem is chosen with intention.
